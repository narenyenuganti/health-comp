Reporting a problem

Include your app version, iOS version, what you were trying to do, the non-sensitive error message, and the approximate time.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, private invitation links, Health screenshots, scores, or unredacted logs.

Missing Activity or delayed points

Check whether your Activity summary is available in Fitness, and review HealthComp’s Health access in Apple’s system settings. A missing summary does not necessarily mean access was denied. No special workout is required.

Do not reinstall or delete your account to troubleshoot a missing update. Contact support if it persists.

Sign-in or connection problems

Keep your app and account state intact. Report the message and approximate time, never your Apple password or a verification code. Do not change your iPhone’s personal iCloud account to test another HealthComp login.

Invitation problems

Share private links only with your intended competitor. Send support the error message, not the link. During an arranged beta competition, do not create a replacement invitation unless the coordinator asks you to.

Privacy and account deletion

For privacy questions or help with deletion, use the contact email above.

To request deletion in HealthComp, tap your profile avatar on the Home screen, then Delete Account. This is different from Sign Out or deleting the app. Complete any Apple reauthorization prompt yourself.

Deletion is permanent when the server workflow completes. Completed shared history remains for your competitor as Former competitor. Contact support if deletion remains pending or you cannot access your account.

HealthComp is a competition app, not a medical service.
