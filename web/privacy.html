HealthComp privacy

HealthComp supports private, seven-day Activity competitions between two people. The initial private beta is invitation-only for a small group of adults in the United States.

Information used by the app

  • Your account: an account identifier and the display name you choose connect you to your competitions. Sign in with Apple and Supabase Auth handle sign-in. Auth account and provider identity records can also contain an email address supplied by Apple, including a private-relay address, and sign-in metadata. Your Apple email-sharing choice affects the information Apple provides.
  • Activity on your iPhone: HealthComp reads Activity summaries to calculate competition scores. It requests related Activity/workout read permissions to notice changes and wheelchair-use information to select Stand/Roll behavior. It does not request permission to write Health data. Raw HealthKit data stays on your iPhone; the score submission does not upload raw workout samples, routes, or raw goal values.
  • Derived competition data: our server stores rounded Activity percentages, Move/Stand/Roll modes, availability, daily points, competition dates/time zone, revision information, results, and awards, linked to participant accounts. Keeping raw Health data on-device does not mean no fitness information is collected by the service.
  • Installation and security information: installation identifiers, notification tokens, and app-integrity verification records support notification delivery, secure score submission, and installation retirement.
  • Operational and support information: the service keeps workflow status and error/event records to operate competitions and account cleanup. If you email support, your email address and the message you choose to send are also received. Our providers process operational information such as authentication events, IP addresses and location inferred from them, browser/device information, and website connection diagnostics. This is separate from competition records and raw HealthKit data.

We use this information to authenticate accounts, calculate and synchronize competitions, deliver notifications, protect score submissions, complete account cleanup, and investigate support requests and service failures.

Sharing and service providers

Your competitor can see your display name and shared competition information, including daily points, availability, dates, results, rounded Move/Exercise/Stand-or-Roll percentages, and Activity modes. These are derived fitness values, not raw HealthKit samples or workout records. A private invitation link grants its recipient a chance to join; share it only with your intended competitor, not in a public issue or support message.

Supabase provides the configured authentication and competition backend. Apple provides sign-in, notification delivery, and app-integrity services. Notification alerts contain generic text, but routing includes a competition identifier and uses an installation's push token. This is not identifier-free traffic. Cloudflare hosts the public website and invitation pages. The site's responses enable browser network-error reporting to Cloudflare; the live support response also includes Cloudflare's email-decoding script. Connection-error reports can include the requested URL and referrer, so avoid placing private information in website URLs or sharing private invitation links publicly. Google Gmail receives mail sent to our support address, including the sender's address and message. We handle support mail in Gmail without automatic forwarding or connected helpdesk, AI, or other support-mail services.

Supabase's customer-data processing terms address its handling of participant data on our behalf. Its separate privacy notice describes the provider's own processing, not a replacement policy for HealthComp's participant records. Other provider notices describe their own practices: Apple, Cloudflare, and Google. Provider processing is not a promise that every copy stays on your iPhone or in one country. The competition database is configured in the U.S. West region; this does not establish the location of all provider processing.

HealthComp's beta does not display advertising, sell participant data, or track participants across other companies' apps or websites. These commitments do not mean that hosting and service providers collect no operational diagnostics.

Controls, deletion, and retained history

You can change HealthComp's Health access and notification permissions through Apple's system controls. Withdrawing access stops the corresponding future access; it does not itself delete previously synchronized competition records.

In HealthComp, tap your profile avatar on the Home screen to open account settings. Sign Out and Delete Account are different: signing out retires this installation and removes this profile's local competition cache after confirmed retirement; it does not delete the account or shared history.

Delete Account starts an authenticated cleanup workflow. When it completes, Apple authorization is revoked, the service authentication identity is removed, active competitions are cancelled, and this profile's local data is cleared. Completed shared results remain for the other participant as Former competitor. The service removes the account linkage and replaces display names in the protected competition history. Revoked installations lose their push tokens; related integrity keys/challenges are purged by the cleanup hooks.

Shared derived results, stable history identifiers, and some operational records remain. The other participant may already know whom they competed with; the label Former competitor does not guarantee that a person is unidentifiable. Deleting the app, signing out, or starting a deletion request is not confirmation that server cleanup completed. Contact support if deletion cannot finish.

Operational records that remain after the cleanup steps above, and support emails, have no fixed automatic deletion schedule. Account deletion and the preservation of shared competition history work as described above. Service providers' logs and backups are separate from these retained records and follow their own practices. Invitation expiry and installation retirement do not automatically erase every related record. Retained shared competition history also has no fixed automatic deletion schedule. We do not promise that a deletion request immediately erases all provider-held copies or backups.
